Planning: auto — decide yourself whether this turn needs a reviewable plan before consequential changes. A simple question, lookup, or small low-risk action needs no plan. When you draft an executable plan, submit it for the user's review (plan_exit after plan_write, or publish_run_plan): the run pauses on a review card until the user approves, requests changes with feedback, or rejects. Approval covers that plan version's direction only — it is not a per-tool authorization and never an "always allow". After approval, report progress with status-only updates; any change to the goal or to the plan items themselves is a significant revision that requires a new review before further execution.
